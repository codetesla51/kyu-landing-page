postgres-backed job queue for go

Stop losing jobs when Redis flushes.

In-memory queues are fast right up until a flush takes your work with it. Postgres-only queues are durable, but slow. Kyu keeps your jobs in Postgres and dispatches them through Redis — durable when it matters, quick where it counts.

the trade-off

Pick two. We built all three.

Every job queue asks you to compromise on something you care about. Kyu was designed to end the compromise.

01 · speed

Fast, but not durable.

In-memory queues move a lot of work very quickly — until a flush, a restart, or a crash turns the entire backlog into a black hole.

02 · safety

Durable, but slow.

Persisting every job keeps it safe, but throughput crawls when the database is fighting the load — and latency balloons under traffic.

03 · stability

Reliable, but heavy.

Kafka and RabbitMQ are dependable. They're also a second infrastructure stack to operate, patch, secure, and pay for.

Kyu is the three-for-three — Postgres durability, in-memory dispatch, and zero new infrastructure.

why kyu

Built for teams that can't lose work.

No DSL, no daemon to operate, no sacrifice act. Just a Go library that treats your jobs like they matter.

durability

Durable by default.

Every job lands in Postgres before it's acknowledged. Redis is the fast lane — never the only place your work exists.

recovery

Survives restarts and flushes.

Jobs in flight are re-queued, not lost. Built-in reapers find stale or orphaned work and put it back where it belongs.

retries

Retries and dead letters.

Automatic retries with backoff, per-job max attempts, and a dead-letter state for the work that's genuinely broken.

performance

Fast where it counts.

Dispatch overhead under a microsecond, and throughput measured past a thousand jobs a second against real Redis.

observability

Observable out of the box.

A built-in dashboard plus Prometheus metrics for depth, processed, failed, and dead jobs — no extra tooling required.

simplicity

A simple Go API.

One dependency, a config struct, and per-team queue isolation. Enqueue from anywhere, process in one place.

quick start

From zero to queued in minutes.

Connect to the Postgres and Redis you already run, register a handler, and start enqueuing work.

install.sh

Requires PostgreSQL and Redis. The jobs table and indexes are created automatically by embedded migrations on first connect.
